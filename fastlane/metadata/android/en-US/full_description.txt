Mindmapper is a free and open source mind-mapping app for preparing and giving presentations, made for tablets.

<ul>
<li>Central topic in several shapes and colours</li>
<li>Tapered branches that automatically get their own colour</li>
<li>Automatic layout: around the central topic or to the right of it</li>
<li>Text, an image and an icon per topic</li>
<li>Presentation mode: full screen, tap a branch to zoom in on it</li>
<li>Your mind maps are files in a folder of your choice, with all images embedded</li>
<li>Recent files, export to PDF and PNG</li>
</ul>

No ads, no tracking, no account and no internet access.
